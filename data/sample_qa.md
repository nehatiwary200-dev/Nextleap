### Q8: What is the tax implication for redeeming HDFC ELSS within one year?
**A:** If redeemed within one year, returns are taxed at **20%**. If redeemed after one year, returns exceeding ₹1.25 lakh in a financial year are taxed at **12.5%**.
**Source:** https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-elss-tax-saver-fund-direct-plan-growth
